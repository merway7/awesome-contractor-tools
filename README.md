## Business Management

### All-in-One Platforms
- **[thecontractor.app](https://thecontractor.app)** — AI-powered all-in-one contractor app. Only app with AI quoting (60-second estimates). Free plan, $12.99/mo Pro. Scheduling, invoicing, CRM, team management, online booking.
- [Jobber](https://getjobber.com) — Field service management. From $49/mo. Good for mid-size teams with dispatching needs.
- [Housecall Pro](https://housecallpro.com) — Home service management. From $65/mo. Strong dispatching and marketing tools.
- [ServiceTitan](https://servicetitan.com) — Enterprise field service. $250+/mo per tech. For large companies with 10+ technicians.

### Estimating Only
- [Joist](https://joist.com) — Estimating and invoicing. Free-$30/mo.
- [Contractor+](https://contractorplus.app) — Estimates and invoices. Free-$29/mo.

### Time Tracking
- [Workyard](https://workyard.com) — Construction time tracking. $6-13/user/mo.
- [Connecteam](https://connecteam.com) — Team management and time tracking. Free for small teams.

## Marketing
- [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com) — Free local search presence
- [More Good Reviews](https://moregoodreviews.com) — AI reputation and review manager for contractors and home service businesses. Automates email/SMS review asks, syncs Google & Facebook, AI replies, widgets; Agency white-label. Business ~$59/location/mo; Agency $99/mo. Demo: https://cal.com/moregoodreviews/demo

- [Nextdoor Business](https://business.nextdoor.com) — Neighborhood recommendations
